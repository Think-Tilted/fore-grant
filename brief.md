A simple, mostly static website for the Grant Golf Tournament.

Core Requirements
1. Sponsor Sign-Up Form
Online registration form for sponsors.
Form includes sponsor tiers.
Each sponsor tier has a finite number of available spots.
Website should automatically indicate when a tier is sold out and prevent additional registrations for that tier.
form data ultimately will be put into a tracker sheet. so would need to talk about the best way to retrieve form submissions. maybe an email?
2. About Grant & The Foundation
Static content page(s) with information about Grant and the foundation.
Includes text, images, and supporting content.
Straightforward content management with minimal maintenance.
3. Optional: Tournament Organization
Keep tournament management within the same site.
Similar to our draft board approach.
Tournament groups, pairings, tee times, and order could be maintained in a spreadsheet.
Website would pull data from the spreadsheet and automatically display updated tournament organization information.
Allows non-technical users to make updates by simply editing the spreadsheet.
goal of this would be something that could be scaffolded and deployed in a minimal amount of time. I would create a figma with come assets, colors, webfonts, and light button/component states. with the goal of simplicity.
